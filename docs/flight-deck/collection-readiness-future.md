# Future archival intake: proposed workflow (design only)

Status: **Proposed future workflow. Not built, not authorized, and not part of Flight Deck 0.1.** It refers to the Memory Record & Relationship Contract 0.2, which is a formal review candidate and is **not adopted**. Nothing here permits collection.
Revision date: 2026-10-16.

Flight Deck 0.1 has no upload route, no file field, and no switch that could enable one. A working CRM does not satisfy any collection gate. Real collection requires the `docs/ethos.txt` §6.1 gates: named operators, approved localized consent and retention procedures, legal and community review, accessibility sign-off, and evidenced security, restore, and withdrawal procedures.

## Proposed sequence

1. **Identity and capacity evidence.** Who is entrusting the material, in what capacity, and on whose authority. Source authority, legal rights, and signing capacity are recorded separately (Manifesto §III). Nothing from a Flight Deck contact record is carried across as evidence: a CRM contact is not a source.
2. **Authorized collection consent.** Use-specific, localized, plain-language, revocable, with disclosed retention and backup windows. Captured in the archive's own consent records, never in Flight Deck.
3. **Quarantine.** Material lands in an isolated store with no public path and no processing.
4. **File validation.** Type and size checks, malware scanning, checksums recorded at intake.
5. **Field completeness.** Required provenance and rights fields present; gaps recorded as gaps, not filled by inference.
6. **Optional, permitted transcription or translation.** Only where the consent covers it, by an approved vendor or person, with no model training or retention by the vendor.
7. **Human rights and sensitivity review.** Restricted, sacred, or contested material identified by people with authority to do so; never by automated classification.
8. **Separate preservation and publication decisions.** Preservation does not imply publication; each use has its own authorization.
9. **Dependency-aware withdrawal.** A withdrawal or restriction propagates to every derived view, copy, and export, with a safe receipt.
10. **Audit receipts.** Minimal, tamper-evident events that never contain the protected content itself.

A synthetic UI prototype of this flow may be built later for review, clearly labeled as a demonstration. It is out of scope for 0.1.
